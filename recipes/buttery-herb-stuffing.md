# Buttery Herb Stuffing

## Ingredients

**Bread**
- 18–24 oz bread cubes (about 1 1/2 loaves, 12–14 cups), stale or toasted

**Aromatics and herbs**
- 1 cup unsalted butter
- 3 cups diced sweet onion (about 2 large)
- 2 cups diced celery
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- Kosher salt and pepper (at least 1/2–1 tsp each)
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh sage
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary

**Liquid**
- 2 1/2 cups chicken or vegetable stock, divided (1 cup + 1 1/2 cups)
- 2 large eggs

**To finish**
- Mixed fresh herbs, chopped

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 dish and put the bread cubes in a large bowl (or right in the dish).
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, celery, garlic, and a big pinch of salt and pepper; cook 8–10 min until soft.
3. Stir in sage, parsley, and rosemary and cook about 1 min more. Add 1 cup of the stock.
4. Pour the vegetable mixture over the bread and toss well.
5. Whisk the remaining 1 1/2 cups stock with the eggs, pour over the bread, and fold until evenly combined. Spread in the dish if you mixed it in a bowl.
6. Bake 45–50 min, until the center reaches 160°F. Tent with foil if the top browns too fast.
7. Sprinkle with fresh herbs to serve.

## Notes

- **Bread:** cube about 1 1/2 lb of bread and leave it loosely covered overnight, or toast the cubes at 350°F about 15 min until crisp, or buy pre-toasted cubes. Mixing two breads (sourdough + Italian) works well.
- **Make ahead:** bake a day ahead and reheat; pull it from the fridge 60 min before reheating.
- **Half batch (serves 4):** halve everything and bake in an 8x8 or 9x9 for the same time.
- **Double batch (serves 12–18):** double everything, use a roasting pan or two 9x13s, and add about 15 min.
- Can also be used to stuff the turkey instead of baking separately.
