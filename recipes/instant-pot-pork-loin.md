# Instant Pot Pork Loin with Pan Juices

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 1/2 lb pork loin roast, boneless or bone-in
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/2 onion, quartered
- 1 1/2 cups unsalted chicken broth or water
- 1 1/2 tbsp cornstarch (optional, to thicken the sauce)

## Steps

1. Take the roast out of the fridge and cut slits in the fat.
2. Mix salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Rub the roast with 1 tbsp olive oil, then coat all sides with the spice mix. Let it rest 45 min.
3. Press Sauté. When the pot reads "Hot," add the remaining oil and the butter.
4. Sear the pork about 3 min each on top and bottom and 1 min on each long side. Press Cancel and move the pork to a plate.
5. Pour in the broth and scrape up all the browned bits.
6. Set the trivet and onion in the pot and place the pork on the trivet, fat side up.
7. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook (Meat/Stew or Pressure Cook) for 19 min.
8. Let it naturally release for 12 min. Check that the pork reads 145°F.
9. Move the pork to a cutting board and take out the trivet.
10. Press Sauté and simmer the juices until reduced by half, 5–7 min. For a thicker sauce, stir in the cornstarch mixed with a little cold water.
11. Slice the pork 1/2 inch thick and serve with the pan juices.

## Notes

- Scrape up every browned bit before pressure cooking, or the pot may throw a burn notice and shut off.
- Not at 145°F yet? Reseal the lid and let residual heat finish it, 2–5 min.
- For a crispy crust, roast the cooked loin on a sheet pan at 475°F for about 10 min.
- This is for pork **loin**, not tenderloin, which cooks differently.
