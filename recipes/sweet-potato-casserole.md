# Sweet Potato Casserole

## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 4 1/2 cups cooked and mashed sweet potatoes
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 eggs, beaten

**Pecan topping**
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup butter
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, mix the mashed sweet potatoes, 1/2 cup melted butter, milk, sugar, vanilla, and eggs. Spread into the prepared dish.
3. In a small bowl, mix brown sugar and flour. Cut in 1/3 cup butter until crumbly, then stir in pecans.
4. Sprinkle the pecan mixture over the sweet potatoes.
5. Bake 25 min, or until golden brown.
